About Us

Welcome to Techbots, where innovation meets inspiration! At our college, we’re not just building robots; we’re crafting the future. Our club is a vibrant community of passionate creators, engineers, and tech enthusiasts united by a shared vision: to push the boundaries of robotics and automation.

Whether you’re a seasoned programmer, a budding engineer, or just curious about technology, Techbots offers a collaborative space to explore ideas, develop skills, and bring projects to life. Together, we’re shaping tomorrow!
